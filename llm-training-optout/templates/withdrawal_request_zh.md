# 撤回训练授权申请 / Withdrawal-of-consent request (China)

Use this when a product has no training toggle, when a toggle can't be found, or when the terms say the only route is customer service (for example CodeBuddy CN §9.4). Fill in the `____` placeholders from information the user gives you. **Never send without the user reviewing the final text.** If the user prefers, prepare the email in a draft and let them send it.

Legal basis [V-m/V]:
- 《个人信息保护法》第十五条: the right to withdraw consent, and the processor must provide a convenient way to do so.
- 《个人信息保护法》第四十七条: deletion after consent is withdrawn.
- GB/T 45654—2025 §6.3: providers must offer a convenient way to stop user inputs being used for training (within 4 clicks), and must prominently tell users how.

---

**邮件主题：** 依据《个人信息保护法》撤回模型训练授权并申请删除 —— 账号 ____

您好：

我是贵方产品「____」的用户，账号/手机号：____（注册邮箱：____）。

根据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》第十五条、第四十七条，以及国家标准 GB/T 45654—2025《网络安全技术 生成式人工智能服务安全基本要求》第6.3条，我现正式：

1. 撤回此前就「将我的输入内容、上传文件、语音及相应生成内容用于模型训练、模型优化或产品改进」所作出的任何同意或授权（包括通过用户协议、默认开关或订购页面勾选等方式作出的授权）；
2. 请求删除或匿名化处理已基于上述授权收集、且尚未用于训练的相关数据；
3. 请书面告知：(a) 上述撤回的生效时间；(b) 产品内是否提供关闭训练的开关及其具体路径；(c) 已删除数据的范围及依法需保留数据的保留依据与期限。

请在法定期限内（贵方隐私政策载明的处理时限）予以回复。如需验证身份，请告知所需材料。

此致
____
____年__月__日

---

**English summary (for the user):** this letter withdraws consent under PIPL Art. 15 and 47 and GB/T 45654-2025 §6.3. It asks the provider to stop training on the user's inputs, uploads, voice and outputs, and to delete data collected under that consent that hasn't been used yet. It also asks the provider to confirm the effective date, any in-product toggle path, and what was deleted or retained and why.
